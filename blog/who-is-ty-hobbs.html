My Story

Nobody Hires a Realtor for His Résumé. Here's Mine Anyway.

By Ty Hobbs  ·  November 6, 2026  ·  3 min read

The bottom line

I'm a Missoula Realtor who played defensive tackle at Montana, taught for five years, and now builds a house or two a year. Every one of those jobs changed how I work for buyers and sellers, and here's how.

Most agent bios read like they were written by the same person. Top producer, passion for people, dedicated to your dreams. You've seen it. So instead of that, here's what actually happened.

Spokane kid, Missoula for good

I grew up in Spokane. Montana recruited me, I came over the mountains to play for the Griz, and I never really left. I played defensive tackle at the University of Montana from 2006 to 2010, and my teammates voted me captain in 2010. I've raised my family here ever since. My three kids are Montanans, and none of them had a choice in the matter.

Defensive tackle is not the glamour position. You work in the middle of the mess so someone else gets to make the play. That turns out to be a decent description of a negotiation, too. A lot of my job is absorbing the pressure so my client doesn't have to.

Five years in a classroom

My degree is in secondary education and business marketing, and I taught for five years before I got into real estate. Teaching ruins you for jargon. If a room of people can't follow what you just said, that's on you, not them. Real estate is full of contracts, deadlines, and acronyms that sound like a foreign language to someone buying their first house or selling their last one. I explain them the way I'd explain them to someone I actually want to understand.

Then I started building

Real estate was always the job. The construction came second, and it changed how I look at everything. I build one to two homes a year, and when they're finished they go on the MLS like any other listing. Working through plans, schedules, budgets, and inspections teaches you how a house actually comes together, and you don't walk through one the old way after that.

Builder's Take

Most people walk into a house and see paint colors, countertops, and staging. A builder sees how the whole house fits together: the layout, how it lives day to day, what it would take to change, and what it's likely worth. I bring that perspective to every showing and every listing, so you're deciding with the full picture.

Who I like working with

My favorite client is the seller who has to buy again. Two big decisions, tied together, with a timeline that has to line up. That's where clear, honest guidance helps the most. Most of my business comes from past clients and people who know me. Missoula is home base, and I serve buyers and sellers across Western Montana.

The gut-check: would I hire me?

Yes, if you want a straight answer, even when that answer is "this might not be the one," or that a big remodel won't always pay you back. I'd rather lose a deal than steer you wrong.

Maybe not, if you're looking for someone to call every house a dream home. I'm going to be honest about what I see, and I'm probably going to ask you a lot of questions.

What this blog is for

Every week I'll put something here that I'd tell you across a kitchen table: what the Missoula-area market is actually doing, what to look for in a house that's easy to overlook, honest guides to the places I sell in, and the occasional story. No fluff, no keyword soup. If there's a question you've been afraid to ask a Realtor, text me. It might be next week's post.
